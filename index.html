WELCOME TO BEATBLOOM

Your first little loop

  1. Choose a groove, then press Play. Tap steps to make it yours.
  2. Use the pads, piano or guitar. Record adds your performance to the pattern.
  3. Import a sound or record up to 30 seconds into a selected sample slot.
  4. Save here for this browser, or download a project file including all samples.

Space: play/pause · 1–4: samples · Ctrl/⌘ Z: undo · Ctrl/⌘ S: save. Switch Piano keys on to use Z–M without pad conflicts.

Microphone access starts only when requested and ends when recording stops. Erase local data removes saved projects and samples. Only share project files with people you want to hear their audio.
